- **Related requirements:** Pending
- **Relevant Brain notes:** Add at least one verified existing note, for example
  `[[Existing note title]]`; do not leave a placeholder in an accepted ADR.

## Context

Describe the actual problem and constraints. Link relevant requirements and source material.

## Decision

Record the decision made by the team. If it is not made, keep the ADR proposed or do not
create the ADR.

## Alternatives considered

List the alternatives actually considered and why they were accepted or rejected.

## Consequences

Describe known benefits, trade-offs, risks, and follow-up work. Distinguish confirmed
consequences from assumptions.

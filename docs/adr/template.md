# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded | Rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decision owners:** Pending
- **Approval record:** Who approved it, when and where (PR review link). Write "Pending" until
  the approval actually exists.
- **Related requirements:** Pending
- **Relevant Brain notes:** At least one existing, distilled note as a relative Markdown link,
  for example [Agent Skills Format](../../brain/resources/Agent-Skills-Format.md). Files under
  `docs/` use relative Markdown links, not Obsidian wikilinks (see ADR 0003).

## Context

Describe the actual problem and constraints. Link relevant requirements and source material.

## Decision

Record the decision made by the team. If it is not made, keep the ADR proposed or do not
create the ADR.

## Alternatives considered

List the alternatives actually considered and why they were accepted or rejected.

## Consequences

Describe known benefits, trade-offs, risks, and follow-up work. Distinguish confirmed
consequences from assumptions.
